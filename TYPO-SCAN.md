The text-extraction step I scanned with converts every HTML tag to whitespace, which silently discards `<iframe>` content. So a video-only module read as "heading + quote, no body." Confirmed the embeds were already present at commit `6fa134c`, the HEAD I scanned against — this was a flaw in my tooling, not a gap in the content. Every playbook has at least one video embed (the Module 1 intro); these two are the only modules where video is the *entire* body, which is why only these two produced a false positive.

## Scan coverage
All 12 playbooks read in full (≈68,000 words of live module text), **plus** the ~9,600 words that now live inside the Key Takeaways and Put It Into Practice graphics — recovered from git history, since that text is no longer greppable in the HTML.

Method: (1) dictionary pass against `/usr/share/dict/words` with rare-token ranking; (2) high-precision regex checks (doubled words, agreement, a/an, its/it's, then/than, punctuation, classic misspellings); (3) structural checks for empty modules, missing closing quotes, and orphaned list items; (4) full manual read of every module.

Real-word errors ("compliment"/"complement", "Crate"/"Create", "their"/"your") are only findable by reading, which is where most of this list came from.
