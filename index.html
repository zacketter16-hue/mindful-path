Meet Your Personal Coach

Hi, I'm Zack Etter

Licensed Therapist, Certified Mental Performance Coach, CEO of Holistic Performance Consulting, Founder of The Mental Fitness Lounge

I'm the CEO of Holistic Performance Consulting, with extensive experience supporting elite performers — from college and professional athletes to Broadway actors, musicians, executives, and first responders.

I lead workshops, provide one-on-one performance coaching, develop Performance Psychology curricula for coaches and organizational leaders, and deliver keynote talks and clinic presentations nationwide.

I'm also the author of My Mental Playbook: The Optimal Performance System for Athletes, which has sold more than 1,000 copies to athletes, coaches, and parents.
